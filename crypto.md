# Stripe Crypto

Integrate one of the Stripe crypto products.

## Build crypto into your Stripe integration

Let your users pay in crypto, pay out in crypto, or embed a fiat-to-crypto onramp.
[Get started with stablecoins](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/stablecoin-payments.md)
## Stablecoins 

[Stablecoin payments](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/stablecoin-payments.md): Let your customers pay with crypto that settles as fiat in your Stripe balance.

[Stablecoin subscriptions (Private preview)](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/stablecoins.md): Authorize recurring stablecoin withdrawals from your customers’ crypto wallets.

[Stablecoins in Treasury (Public preview)](https://docs.stripe.com/treasury/stablecoins.md): Receive, store, and send funds globally with stablecoins in your Stripe Financial Account.

## Stablecoins for platforms and marketplaces 

[Stablecoin payouts with Connect (Private preview)](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/stablecoin-payouts.md): Pay out in stablecoins to connected accounts from platforms and marketplaces.

[Stablecoin-backed card issuing with Connect (Private preview)](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/stablecoin-cards-for-financial-accounts.md): Issue virtual or physical prepaid cards for connected accounts, backed by stablecoin balances.

### Interested in getting early access to stablecoins in your Treasury for platforms integration?

Enter your email to request access.

```bash
curl https://docs.stripe.com/preview/register \
  -X POST \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Referer: https://docs.stripe.com/crypto" \
  -d '{"email": "EMAIL", "preview": "treasury_for_platforms_stablecoin_interests_preview"}'
```

## Fiat-to-crypto onramp 

[Use the Stripe-hosted onramp (Public preview)](https://docs.stripe.com/crypto/onramp/stripe-hosted.md): Customize and generate a redirect URL to the Stripe-hosted, standalone hosted onramp.

[Build an Embedded onramp integration (Public preview)](https://docs.stripe.com/crypto/onramp/embedded-quickstart.md): Build an Embedded onramp integration using our interactive, code-based quickstart that includes downloadable files.

[Embedded Components integration (Private preview)](https://docs.stripe.com/crypto/onramp/embedded-components.md): Learn how to sign up for the Embedded Components onramp, a customizable Onramp SDK that supports Web (Javascript) and Mobile (iOS, Android, and React Native).
