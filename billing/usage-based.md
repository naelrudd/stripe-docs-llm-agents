# Usage-based billing

Charge customers based on how much they use your product or service.

## 

Metronome, a Stripe product, is a usage-based billing platform that provides real-time metering, pricing, billing, and reporting. Use Metronome to ingest usage events, define flexible pricing models, and automate invoice generation without building custom billing infrastructure.
[Try Metronome](https://signup.metronome.com/)
