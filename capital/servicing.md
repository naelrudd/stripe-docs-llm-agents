# Managing customer support

Support your Capital customers by region.

> Capital for platforms is available in [public preview](https://docs.stripe.com/release-phases.md).

Capital for Platforms is available in the following countries. Servicing arrangements vary by country based on the financing partner.

- AU
- DE
- FR
- GB
- US

You must submit any created content or material changes to approved support content through the [Change Request Form](https://form.asana.com/?k=8K51UWmWhttehNFD5qBLdg&d=974470123217835) in Google Doc or PDF format. Stripe’s product legal team, compliance team, and banking partner must approve all user-facing materials before you can send to users.

If your support team chooses to field basic, frontline user inquiries, we offer pre-approved, compliant training materials and templated responses. Contact your Stripe account team or Stripe Support to request templates.

Select your country to learn how to provide servicing to your connected accounts.

#### US

### United States

Stripe is the first line of support for your users’ inquiries. Every communication includes a support email [capital+support@stripe.com](mailto:capital+support@stripe.com) users can contact if they have questions. If a user contacts your platform with questions or concerns regarding their financing, forward the user and their questions to the Stripe team for support.

Stripe handles the following servicing functions:

- Reviewing applications
- Collecting payments
- Maintaining records of payments and balances
- General administration of the financing
- Responding to borrower inquiries
- Following up on delinquencies

#### GB

### United Kingdom

## Escalate complaints

In every region, escalate all complaints to [capital+support@stripe.com](mailto:capital+support@stripe.com). Stripe has a legal obligation to report all Capital-related complaints. To learn how to manage complaints, see [Regulatory compliance](https://docs.stripe.com/capital/regulatory-compliance.md).

Trigger words that indicate a complaint include:

- Angry
- Attorney
- Deceived
- Discrimination
- Lawsuit
- Litigation
- Mad
- Mislead
- Predatory
- Sue
- Unfair
